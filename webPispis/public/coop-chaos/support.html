Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Move with the left joystick. Drag the world to look around. Use Jump, Peck and Dash to explore and interact with props. The clipboard button shows challenges.

Does the game need internet?

No. The game works offline and does not require an account. Email support and this website need an internet connection.

Where is my progress saved?

Exploration progress saves automatically on your device. Feather Frenzy has a separate local best score. There is no in-game cloud sync or transfer between devices.

My chicken is stuck.

Open the pause menu and use the return-to-coop button. You can also adjust sound and haptics there.

How do I start again?

Choose “Start a new farm” in the pause menu and confirm. This replaces exploration progress; your best score and settings remain.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later, in landscape. The game follows your device language and supports English and Turkish.

How do I report a problem?

Email your device model, iOS version, app version and the steps that caused the issue. Attach a screenshot if helpful, without personal or sensitive information.
