Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026 · App version 1.0

Coop Chaos does not collect or transmit personal data through the game. It works offline, without accounts, advertisements, analytics or in-app purchases.

Who this policy covers

This policy explains the data practices of Coop Chaos (Chicken Simulator), developed by Salih Bekaroğlu, for iPhone and iPad. It also explains what happens when you contact support or visit this website.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your exploration progress, collected items, challenge progress, in-game position, best Feather Frenzy score, and sound and haptic settings locally using iOS storage. These records are used to resume your game and remember your preferences. The app does not send them to us or to a server.

Collection, sharing and tracking

The game has no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location. We do not receive, sell or share gameplay data. There is no account registration or developer-operated cloud save.

Deleting saved progress

“Start a new farm” in the pause menu resets exploration progress. It does not clear your best score or settings. To remove all local app data, delete the app using iOS; offloading an app preserves its data. Device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings, and restoring a backup may restore saved data.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address; information required to meet legal obligations may need to be retained.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children. A parent or guardian can contact us about privacy or support.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its service. This is separate from the offline game. See GitHub’s privacy statement. These Coop Chaos pages do not include analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when our data practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact Salih Bekaroğlu at genezinsalih@gmail.com.
