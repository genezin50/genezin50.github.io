Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap an ice cream cart to move it in the direction of its arrow. Its path must be clear. Customers circle the belt and are served by a cart of the same color at a service bay. A cart leaves after serving its stock. Keep bays available for the next colors.

A cart is not moving. What should I do?

Check its arrow, the carts in its path and the available service bays. Some puzzles add special rules; open “How to play” in the pause menu to read them or try a practice puzzle. You can use Hint or Undo from the bottom controls, or restart the current day from the pause menu.

Does everything work offline?

All game puzzles, practice modes and local rewards work without an internet connection. No account is required. Website pages and email support require a connection. The game does not automatically download extra content.

Where is my progress saved?

The game saves progress automatically on this device. There is no account or automatic game cloud sync. Before deleting the app or moving devices, export a backup if you want to keep your progress.

How do I make or restore a backup?

Open the pause button at the top left, then choose “Save a backup to Files” under “Offline backup”. Keep the JSON file in a safe location. On the destination device, open Scoop Street and choose “Restore from a backup”, then select that file. Restoring replaces the destination’s current progress. A cloud storage location may need internet access.

Are there paid coins, ads or online opponents?

This version has no ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Coins and rewards are earned locally through play. Characters in events are computer-controlled; there is no online matchmaking or shared leaderboard.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Scoop Street supports iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17 or later, in portrait. The game follows your preferred device language and supports English and Turkish.

How do I report a bug?

Email your device model, iOS version, app version, puzzle/day number and the steps that caused the issue. Mention whether it happened in your route, archive or practice. A screenshot can help; remove personal information before sending it. Please avoid deleting the app before making a backup.
