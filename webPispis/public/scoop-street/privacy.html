Scoop Street

Privacy policy

Your game stays on your device.

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Scoop Street for iOS and iPadOS

Scoop Street works offline. The game does not collect or send personal data to the developer, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Who we are

Scoop Street is developed by Salih Bekaroğlu. For privacy questions, contact genezinsalih@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your puzzle progress, current session, game coins, booster inventory, earned rewards, selected cosmetics and sound/haptic preferences locally. The device date is used for local daily rewards and seasonal activities. This information is used to run and restore your game; it is not sent to a developer server.

There is no account, online multiplayer, online leaderboard or automatic in-game cloud sync. Any competitors shown in local activities are computer-controlled characters.

Backups you choose to make

You can export a JSON backup through the game’s menu to a location you choose in Apple’s Files interface. You can also select a backup file to restore your progress. Importing a backup replaces the current saved progress.

The game does not upload these backups itself. If you choose iCloud Drive or another storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Backups contain game progress and settings; share them only with people you trust.

Permissions, ads and purchases

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not use advertising identifiers, show ads, sell personal information or track activity across apps or websites. Game coins are earned through gameplay; this version has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate reported problems. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and any related obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence by emailing the address above. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Managing and deleting data

Saved game data remains on your device until it is replaced or removed. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Files you exported are separate: delete them from the location you selected if you no longer want them. Device backups and any files held by a storage provider are controlled through that provider or your Apple settings. We cannot recover local progress from a developer server.

Children’s privacy

The game does not require an account, personal information or chat. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. A parent or guardian can contact us about a privacy concern or request deletion of information sent in a support message.

This website and external links

These game pages use no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening this website or sending email requires internet access; playing the game does not.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Contact us using the address above for questions or privacy requests.
