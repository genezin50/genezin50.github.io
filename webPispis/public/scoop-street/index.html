Actual gameplay · iPhone

Follow the arrows

One tap. A little planning.

Every cart has a direction. Every customer has a color. Make room for the right match.

1

Open a route.

Tap a cart to move it along its arrow. Clear the carts blocking its path first.

2

Match the crowd.

Customers travel around the belt. Park the matching color at the service bays to serve them.

3

Keep things moving.

Once a cart has served its stock, it leaves. Plan your next arrival before the bays fill up.
